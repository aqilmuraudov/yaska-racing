## English

**Name:** Yaska Racing

**Short description (80 chars):**
Kart racing in Baku! Flame Towers, Maiden Tower, the Boulevard - drift and win!

**Full description:**
Yaska and her friends race through the streets of Baku!

🏁 Three cities: the Seafront (Boulevard, State Flag Square, Heydar Aliyev Center), the Skyscraper city (28 Mall, Port Baku, Park Bulvar) and the Old City (Maiden Tower, Qosha Qala gate).
🚗 7 lovable racers and their karts: Yaska, HavHav, Posi, Bichok, Leloyun, Mestan, Chepish.
🎙️ The racers talk in Azerbaijani.
🌅 Day, sunset and night races - the Flame Towers light up at night!
🎁 Gift boxes, drift boosts, collect stars and manat, paint and tune your kart in the garage.
🏆 Baku Cup, Time Trial against your own ghost, a daily challenge and a sticker album.
👶 "Auto" mode for little ones: the kart drives itself. The "Learn" button shows every button step by step.

No ads. No purchases. No internet needed. No data collected.
